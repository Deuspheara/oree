Oree / Android TV launcher

A quieter home
for Android TV.

Oree brings your apps, favorites, and household profiles together in a clean, local-first TV launcher.

Personal spaces

A home for everyone.

Switch between local household profiles, each with its own favorites, layout and appearance.

Your streaming apps keep their own accounts.

Everyday navigation

Made for your remote.

Move, select, open.

Navigate with the arrows, select an app to open it, and hold for app actions.

Keep favorites close.

Arrange your dock, group apps in folders, and use local search to find an app.

Start with your apps.

Open installed apps offline, without a mandatory Oree account, advertising or telemetry.
