Questions, answered.

Can I download Oree yet?

Oree is a development preview. There is no downloadable release yet. If you want to try it, the build guide explains how to build and install the app.

Open the build guide ↗
Does Oree replace my streaming apps?

Oree is a launcher for Android TV and Google TV. It opens your installed apps; those apps still handle playback, subscriptions and their own accounts.

Do I need an account or photo access?

No Oree account or media permission is needed to launch apps. Photos and provider content are optional, and the app-only Home remains useful without them.

Is the source available?

Yes. Oree is free and open source under Apache-2.0. You can inspect the implementation, build it yourself and contribute on GitHub.
